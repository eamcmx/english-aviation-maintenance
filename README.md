# English for Aviation Maintenance (CEFR B2)

A complete **32-unit English for Specific Purposes course** for aircraft mechanics and avionics
technicians working to **EASA Part-66 / Part-145**, produced end to end with **CourseForge LANG**.

**▶ Live demo: https://eamcmx.github.io/english-aviation-maintenance/**

## What the units cover

Documentation and task cards, tech-log defects, shift handovers, human factors (the Dirty Dozen),
stores and AOG parts, troubleshooting, BITE readouts, wiring diagrams, Service Bulletins and
Airworthiness Directives, MEL dispatch, component removal and installation, inspection findings,
Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) writing, functional tests, OEM support calls, avionics
software loads, incident reporting, planning meetings, and **certifying release to service**,
with 8 full simulation days and a capstone.

## How it was made

Every unit was written, levelled and packaged with **CourseForge LANG**, our in-house authoring
system that turns a specification (level, specialism, ECTS) into complete teaching materials.
Quality is enforced by measurement, not by review:

- **CEFR band gate.** Every sentence is scored (Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog, SMOG, Coleman-Liau). Text that is too hard *or too easy* fails.
- **Volume gate.** A unit must carry enough material for a full 90-minute class or it is rejected.
- **Vocabulary discipline.** Every bank term must be practised in the exercises *and* spoken in a dialogue.
- **Audio gate.** Every listening track must exist, play, and fall inside its duration window.

Audio: Google Gemini dialogue TTS. Design: a single deterministic design system applied to every unit.

CourseForge LANG is not distributed as software. Courses like this one are produced as a service.
