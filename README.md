# English for Aviation Maintenance — CEFR B2

A complete **32-unit English for Specific Purposes course** for aircraft mechanics and avionics
technicians working to **EASA Part-66 / Part-145**, generated end to end with **CourseForge LANG**.

**▶ Live demo: https://eamcmx.github.io/english-aviation-maintenance/**

| | |
|---|---|
| Level | CEFR B2 (level-controlled by measurement, not by opinion) |
| Volume | 6 ECTS · 64 contact hours · 32 units of 90 minutes |
| Per unit | 3 dialogues + 1 announcement (studio audio) · 18–22 specialist terms · 8 exercises with 60+ self-checking items · 2 reading texts · 4 role-plays · 1 writing task |
| Teacher pack | classroom deck · full answer key with rationale · staging and timing notes · L1 interference guidance (Latvian/Russian) · CEFR speaking rubric |
| Assessment | a skills-split quiz per unit (listening, reading, use of English, vocabulary) in Moodle XML |

## What the units cover

Documentation and task cards · tech-log defects · shift handovers · human factors (the Dirty Dozen) ·
stores and AOG parts · troubleshooting · BITE readouts · wiring diagrams · Service Bulletins and
Airworthiness Directives · MEL dispatch · component removal/installation · inspection findings ·
Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) writing · functional tests · OEM support calls · avionics
software loads · incident reporting · planning meetings · **certifying release to service** —
with 8 full simulation days and a capstone.

## How it was made

Every unit was written, levelled and packaged by [CourseForge LANG](https://github.com/eamcmx/courseforge-dilc),
a desktop application that turns a specification (level, specialism, ECTS) into complete teaching
materials. Quality is enforced by measurement, not review:

- **CEFR band gate** — every sentence scored (Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog, SMOG, Coleman-Liau); text that is too hard *or too easy* fails.
- **Volume gate** — a unit must carry enough material for a full 90-minute class or it is rejected.
- **Vocabulary discipline** — every bank term must be practised in the exercises *and* spoken in a dialogue.
- **Audio gate** — every listening track must exist, play and fall within its duration window.

Audio: Google Gemini dialogue TTS. Design: a single deterministic design system applied to every unit.
