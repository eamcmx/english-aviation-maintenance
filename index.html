What makes this different

Not a textbook adapted for aviation: materials written for the work these technicians actually do.

Built for the hangar, not the classroom

Every unit works a real EASA Part-145 situation: task cards, tech-log defects, BITE readouts, MEL dispatch, release to service.

Four skills in every unit

Listening from studio-quality dialogues, vocabulary in context, grammar taught from the dialogues, staged practice, role-plays and a written deliverable.

Level-controlled by measurement

Every sentence is scored (Flesch, Fog, SMOG, Coleman-Liau) and must sit inside the CEFR band: too hard and too easy both fail the gate.

Real audio, not read-aloud text

Two-voice studio dialogues generated for each unit, embedded in the page with transcripts revealed after listening.

Teacher-ready out of the box

Each unit ships a classroom deck, full answer key with rationale, staging and timing notes, L1 interference guidance and a CEFR speaking rubric.

Assessment included

A skills-split quiz per unit (listening, reading, use of English, vocabulary) exports straight into Moodle.

Click any unit to open the real student lesson: audio plays, exercises check themselves. Teacher deck, answer key and quiz ship with every unit.
