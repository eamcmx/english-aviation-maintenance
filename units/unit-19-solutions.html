Add the missing articles

#AnswerWhy (article case)
1the / theKnown parts — the reader knows which bolt and which bracket.
2aFirst mention of a crack — one of many, not a special one.
3aFirst mention of a new seal — any one new seal.
4theA specific, known system — the left hydraulic system.
5the / theBoth are known: the warning and the step already referred to.

Writing task · model answer (annotated)

Rewrite the shift note as a defect report

Six to eight short lines, in Simplified Technical English. Each line below is followed by the reason it meets the standard.

Defect There is corrosion on the antenna bracket on the right side.

A checkable fact, not a guess. Approved word "corrosion". Articles present: the antenna bracket.

Defect The corrosion is near the lower edge, close to the bolt hole.

Location given precisely; no noun cluster — "the bolt hole", not "antenna bracket bolt hole".

Action The technician cleaned the surface. The corrosion is still visible.

One idea per sentence, active voice, past facts of what was done and seen.

Action The technician did not torque the loose bolt, because the torque wrench was not available.

States the limit of the work honestly; the reason is a fact the next shift can act on.

State The bracket is unserviceable until an inspector checks it.

Approved word "unserviceable" — not "bad" or "okay". The part's state is explicit.

Caution Do not apply load to the antenna before the bolt is torqued to the correct value.

Only the part is at risk here → a caution. A command in the active voice.

Admin Record this in the tech log and keep the work order open.

Closes the loop: the record and the work order both follow the report.

Self-check applied Every sentence is short (one idea). Every noun has the right article. Every word is an approved word with one meaning. The state is given, with a caution where the part can be damaged.
