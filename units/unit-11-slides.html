01 · The situation

The gear-down lamp stays off

A landing-gear warning snag, written in the tech log by the crew. Your job this shift: trace the conductor on the WDM, find the connector and pin on the mock harness, and describe the route so your partner locates it.

One reads the diagram. One locates the parts and reads every reference back. Unless the words match exactly, the wrong wire gets disconnected.

Elicit before you play the audio

  • What could make a warning lamp stay dark?
  • Where does a wire begin and end on a diagram?
  • How do we name a connector so nobody confuses it?

Collect answers on the board; you will return to them after Dialogue 1.

02 · Listening · Dialogue 2

Calling references on a mock harness

The read-back routine

A calls a reference → B locates it → B reads the whole reference back.

"Connector P4, pin 7, top row of the socket, next to the shielded conductor."

Why: one digit wrong and the splice ends up in the wrong position.

Gist task: What does Technician B find wrong on the run? (chafed insulation near the clamp)

Drill this frame chorally, then in pairs:

"Say the whole reference back to me."
"Connector ___, pin ___, ___ row, next to ___."

This dialogue is the rehearsal for role-play card 1.

03 · Vocabulary · anatomy of a connection

Connector, plug, receptacle, pin, socket, contact

The whole fitting

connector — joins two sets of wires; has two halves.
plug — the half you push in · receptacle — the fixed half on the unit.

The contacts inside

pin — the male contact · socket — the female cavity that receives it.
contact — the general word for the metal part that carries current.

mate the connectormating plugreceptacle endsocket contactbacked-out contact

Check: which half is fixed to the structure? → the receptacle.

03 · Vocabulary · fault & test words

Continuity, open, short, resistance, ground

The path

continuity unbroken path
open circuit complete break
short circuit unwanted path to ground

Measuring

resistance opposition, in ohms
multimeter reads V, Ω, continuity
insulation the protective cover

Structure return

ground return to airframe
bonding low-resistance link
shield · splice · routing

Learn the collocation with the word: continuity test · short circuit to ground · measure resistance · bonding strap · shield braid · wire routing.

04 · Grammar · Latvian & Russian slips

Three errors to pre-empt

Doubled "to"

runs to P1 to J12
runs from P1 to J12

LV/RU mark start & end the same way — English needs from … to.

"in" a pin

the contact is in pin 3
on pin 3 / at pin 3

Russian «в» pulls to in; a contact sits on/at.

"trough" + no "the"

goes trough harness
through the harness

No articles in LV/RU → the dropped; spelling of through.

Recast, don't lecture: say the right form, learner repeats, move on. These three return in exercise E6.

07 · Writing · tech-log entry (STE)

Corrective action in Simplified Technical English

Task: a corrective-action entry, ~70–100 words. How you traced the conductor, what the continuity test showed, the action you took.

STE habits:

  • Short sentences — one idea each.
  • Start each step with an action verb: Traced… Set… Inspected… Re-seated… Confirmed…
  • Name the connector, the pin and the path.

Self-check (4 lines)

  • Every sentence short, one idea.
  • Connector, pin and path named.
  • from … to for the conductor path.
  • Test result + what I did next.

Draft in class, finish for homework. Annotated model in solutions.html.
