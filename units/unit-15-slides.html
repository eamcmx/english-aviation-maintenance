§1 · the situation

Night at the line station — can we go?

An A320 is on stand, ready to fly. The crew wrote up a fault in the technical log. One item is inoperative, and the turnaround clock is running.

You do not decide alone. You call the duty engineer, open the Minimum Equipment List, read the conditions, and agree the go or no-go. Then you fit the placard, defer the defect, and sign the release.

§3 · Listening · Dialogue 1

One air-conditioning pack inoperative

Listen twice. Books closed first.

1 · Which pack is inoperative, and what is the fault code?

2 · What repair category is it, and how many days?

3 · What is the altitude limitation, and what must you fit?

Play from the unit page (Dialogue 1). Answers: number two pack, flow-control valve fails to open; category C, ten calendar days; not above FL310, an "INOP" placard by the switch.

§3 · Interactive · rAF · the altitude limitation

Why FL310 — a single-pack limitation

Drag the flight level. One pack can pressurise the cabin only up to FL310.

The aircraft may dispatch, but the crew must not climb above the limitation. Read the whole sentence aloud with the class.

§3 · Listening · Dialogue 2

Weather radar inoperative — and a missing part

Listen for two lists.

1 · What does the MEL require for the radar?

2 · Where does the missing static wick go — MEL or CDL?

3 · Which item needs a placard, and which does not?

Radar: avoid thunderstorms (the operational procedure), placard, defer. Static wick: Configuration Deviation List, no placard, but record it in the technical log.

§3 · Listening · Dialogue 3 + handover

APU inoperative — is it a no-go item?

Decide before the engineer does.

1 · Is a failed APU automatically a no-go item?

2 · Which proviso turns it into a go item?

3 · How does the crew start the engines without the APU?

Not automatic; category B, three days. Go item if a ground air source is available; engine start by cross-bleed or air-cart. Then play the handover voicemail as a model recap.

§2 · Interactive · rAF · repair categories

Category → rectification interval

Pick a repair category. The bar shows how many calendar days you have to fix it.

B = 3 days (the APU). C = 10 days (the pack and the radar). The clock starts the moment you defer the defect.

§4 · grammar in context · modals

Permission & possibility — the modals dispatch runs on

FunctionModalFrom the calls
Permission (allowed)may / canThe aircraft may dispatch with one pack inoperative.
Possibility (maybe)could / may / mightThe fault could be in the valve.
Prohibition (not allowed)must not / may notThe flight must not climb above FL310.
Future abilitywill be able toWe will be able to fix it tomorrow.

Rule: a modal takes the bare verb — may dispatch, never may to dispatch — and modals never stack (will can ✗).

§5 · Interactive · rAF · the dispatch decision

Go or no-go — walk the logic

Flip each gate and watch the decision. All three green → the aircraft may dispatch.

If a proviso is unmet, it must not go until it is met. Narrate each path with the right modal.

§7 · speaking · four role-plays

Hold the call — technician and duty engineer

Card 1

Landing light inoperative, night departure — agree category, interval, placard.

Card 2

Anti-skid unserviceable, wet runway — go or no-go, and why.

Card 3

One VHF radio inoperative — read the MEL item together, list the conditions.

Card 4

Shift handover — the item, the limitation, the expiry of the interval.

Swap roles each card. Recast the modal and article errors softly. Score with the B2 rubric in the key.

§8 · writing · Simplified Technical English

Write a deferred-defect entry

Task: VHF 2 is inoperative on an A320. MEL category C, ten days. Proviso: one VHF works and the crew is briefed.

State the defect, the MEL reference, the limitation, the placard, and both procedures. Finish with the release. 90–120 words.

STE habits: short sentences (under ~20 words) · one instruction each · active voice · approved words.

Start it in class. The annotated model answer is in the solutions file — reveal it after they draft.
