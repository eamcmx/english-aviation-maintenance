WWriting task — annotated model answer

Tech-log entry, 60–90 words, Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100 habits). Formal register.

Fault: BITE readout showed "TP12 open". Finding: test pin TP12 in connector P12 had no connection. The task-card reference was misread at first. Action: called the OEM support desk. The engineer clarified that P12 is the connector and TP12 is the test pin. Confirmed the part number against the wiring diagram. Read back both codes in the phonetic alphabet. Rectification: reseated the connector and re-ran the BITE test. The readout is now clear.

Why this scores well — (1) Order: fault → finding → action → rectification, the expected shape of a log entry. (2) STE habits: one idea per sentence, every sentence under 20 words, plain past (reseated, confirmed), no passive, the same word for the same thing (connector, test pin). (3) Confirmation shown: the paraphrase and the phonetic read-back are both recorded, so a reader sees how meaning was checked. (4) Register: formal throughout — no mate, no contractions in the record, no hangar slang. Word count ≈ 78.
