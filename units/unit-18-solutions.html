E6 Error-spotting — find and fix eight errors

Skill: correcting the exact Latvian/Russian comparative errors — double comparative, or the wrong word for "than".

#Fix (type in the box)Why
1longerDouble comparative: never more with an -er word.
2thanComparison uses than, not as.
3thanthan (comparison), not then (time).
4shorterNeeds the comparative form shorter, not the base word.
5deeperShort adjective → deeper, never more deep.
6thanAfter more serious the link word is than, not as.
7smallerShort adjective → smaller, never more small.
8biggerShort adjective, double the g → bigger, never more big.
Board round-up: two error families only — (a) double marking (more longer → longer) and (b) wrong link word (as/from/then → than). Fix the family, not the single word.

✍︎ Model writing answer — combustion-chamber finding report

Task: a 5 mm crack on a liner panel (limit 4 mm) plus light corrosion within the blend limit. 100–130 words, Simplified Technical English, a comparative with than, and a recommendation with a reason.

BORESCOPE INSPECTION REPORT — COMBUSTION CHAMBER

Inspection: borescope inspection of the combustion chamber.

Finding 1: a crack on the leading edge of a liner panel. Dimension: 5 mm. Severity: the crack is clear and open.

Assessment: the serviceable limit is 4 mm. The crack is longer than the limit, so it is out of limits.

Recommendation: refer the crack to engineering, because it is out of limits.

Finding 2: light corrosion on a nearby bracket, found by visual inspection. The pitting is shallow.

Assessment: the corrosion is within the blend limit.

Recommendation: repair it by blending, then protect the surface. Accept it after the blend.

Status: engine not released until engineering answers on Finding 1.

≈ 108 words

How this answer scores — annotated against the task:

  • ✓ Tool named — "borescope inspection of the combustion chamber" opens the report.
  • ✓ Type · location · dimension · severity — Finding 1 gives all four in order (crack / leading edge of a liner panel / 5 mm / clear and open).
  • ✓ Comparative with "than" — "The crack is longer than the limit" carries the decision; no double comparative, correct link word.
  • ✓ Within / out of limits stated — "out of limits" for the crack; "within the blend limit" for the corrosion.
  • ✓ Recommendation + reason — "refer … because it is out of limits"; "repair by blending … accept after the blend".
  • ✓ Simplified Technical English — one idea per sentence, short sentences, active voice ("The borescope finds…", not "It was observed that…").
  • ✓ Length — inside the 100–130 word window.

Mark down if a student: writes "the crack is more longer than…" (double comparative); uses "as/from/then" for "than"; gives a decision with no reason; or omits the dimension or severity of a finding.

Full-marks minimum: both findings described in order, one correct comparative with than, both "within/out of limits" labels, and a reasoned recommendation for each finding.
