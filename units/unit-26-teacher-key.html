Unit staging & timing (90 minutes)

Latvian & Russian interference — why each error happens

These are predictable, not careless. Naming the first-language cause helps learners self-correct, so give the reason, not only the fix.

Grammar (the unit's focus)

L1Typical errorCorrectWhy it happens
LV RU"The mechanic which found the dent…"The mechanic who found the dent…Latvian kas and Russian который serve for both people and things, so learners under-use "who".
LV RU"The dent, that exceeds the limit,…"The dent that exceeds the limit… (no commas)Neither L1 marks the defining / non-defining split with the same comma logic, so commas are placed by feel.
RU"The dent, which it exceeds the limit…"The dent, which exceeds the limit…Russian relative clauses can keep a resumptive pronoun; learners copy the extra "it".
LV RU"This is the zone which the loader hit the skin."…the zone where the loader hit the skin.Both L1s often use the general relative word for places too, so "where" is skipped.

Articles, aspect & prepositions

L1Typical errorCorrectWhy it happens
LV RU"There is dent in skin."There is a dent in the skin.Neither Latvian nor Russian has articles, so a/the are dropped or added at random.
RU"I measure the dent already." (for a done action)I have measured the dent already.Russian marks completion with aspect, not a perfect tense, so the present perfect is under-used.
LV RU"It is out of limit." / "with limits"It is out of limits / within limits.The fixed English plurals and prepositions do not map one-to-one from L1.
RU"depth 1,4 mm" (comma decimal)depth 1.4 mm (point decimal, said "one point four")LV/RU write the decimal comma; English maintenance uses the point and says "point".

Pronunciation & near-pairs

SoundWatch forWhy
gauge vs gouge/ɡeɪdʒ/ (tool) vs /ɡaʊdʒ/ (deep mark)Near-homophones with opposite jobs; drill in minimal pairs and in the E6 spelling item.

Extension activities

1 · Number dictation (fast, 4 min). Read ten mixed figures at speaking pace — zone 127, station 480, 1.4 mm, 2.6 mm, 40 mm, station 720, zone 300, 1.0 mm, 2.0 mm, stand 5. Learners write them, then read them back. Targets the real skill of catching spoken figures.
2 · Turn the report into a PA notice. Give pairs the E7 report form; they rewrite it as a 6-line hangar PA like Dialogue 4. Bridges written STE and spoken register.
3 · Defining vs non-defining sort. Ten sentences on cards; learners sort into "identifies which one" (no commas, that/which) vs "adds extra detail" (commas, which). Then they add or remove the commas.
4 · Referral or repair? decision cards. Six mini-cases (depth + location + corrosion yes/no). Pairs decide within limits / out of limits and repair / referral, and must state the reason in one relative-clause sentence.
5 · Genre swap (stronger groups). Compare the SRM page (E5) with the report (E7): which is instructions, which is a record? Learners label the verbs (imperatives vs past facts) — a light AMM-vs-tech-log genre awareness task.
Homework: finish the STE Ground Damage Report (writing task) and complete the Moodle quiz. Bring one real or invented ground-damage case to describe next lesson.

CEFR B2 speaking rubric — the role-plays

Mark each learner across five criteria (0–4). B2 sits at band 3. Use it live during cards 1–4; give two praise points and one fix per learner.

Criterion4 · Above B23 · B2 (target)2 · Approaching1 · Below
Range Uses bank terms and relative clauses flexibly; precise, varied phrasing. Uses the unit's specialist terms and relative clauses correctly to specify damage and location. Some bank terms; leans on a few fixed phrases; little subordination. Very limited vocabulary; cannot describe location or extent.
Accuracy Errors rare and self-repaired; who/which/that and commas controlled. Good control; relative words mostly right; slips do not block meaning. Frequent article/relative errors that sometimes obscure which dent is meant. Errors dense; the report cannot be trusted.
Fluency Smooth, near-natural pace; hesitation only for real thought. Keeps going at a steady pace; some pausing to plan, but sustained. Halting; long pauses; needs prompting to continue. Cannot maintain the exchange.
Interaction Manages the exchange; asks for and gives the exact zone/station/depth without prompting. Initiates and responds; checks the number and location; hands over clearly. Mostly responds; waits to be asked for details. Does not sustain turn-taking.
Coherence Logical build: location → measurement → decision → reason, well linked. Clear order; uses connectors (because, although, unless) to justify the decision. Some sequencing; the justification is thin or missing. Ideas disconnected; no line of reasoning.
Total/20  ·  15 = solid B2  ·  a card is "passed" when the learner locates by zone/station, compares to a limit, and gives a reason.
