§0 · warm-up · the situation

Do not open the task until you know

SB 34-2189 arrives. The effectivity wording is unclear. Is serial 5127 on the list? A wrong reading costs hours, orders parts you do not need, or leaves a fault open.

1 · NAME IT

Give the bulletin and its revision.

2 · IDENTIFY

Read out your serial number.

3 · ASK

Ask your precise questions.

4 · CONFIRM

Repeat back, agree the next step.

§1 · Dialogue 1 · listening

"Is my aircraft affected?"

TECHIt is SB 34-2189, revision 2. Could you tell me which effectivity applies?
OEMDo you have your serial number ready?
TECHOur serial number is 5127. Could you confirm whether this bulletin is mandatory?
OEMRecommended, not mandatory. Serial 5127 is inside the effectivity. Your aircraft is affected.

Listen 1 (books closed): affected? mandatory? · Listen 2 (books open): underline the two indirect questions.

§1 · Dialogue 2 · listening for figures

"Mandatory, and is there a deadline?"

ENGIs this bulletin linked to an airworthiness directive?
OEMYes. The AD makes the modification mandatory. Revision 3 is current; revision 2 is superseded.
ENGThe first serial number is 4402. And is there a deadline for compliance?
OEMSerial 4402 is affected. Compliance is due within twelve months of the AD date.

Catch the figures: 4402 · rev 3 current · rev 2 superseded · 12 months. Spoken numbers are a real skill.

§1 · Dialogue 3 + voicemail · confirming

"Let me confirm the next step"

TECHFirst we remove the bracket. Then we install the new part number. Is that right?
OEMCorrect. Then inspect the area before you close the panel. No rework of the wiring.
TECHWhat is the lead time for the kit?
OEMAbout three weeks. Order the kit, embody the mod, then make the logbook entry.

Voicemail (extra): revision 2 is now superseded; revision 3 is current; two more serials are affected; still recommended.

Ask the room: what must you check before you start? → the new effectivity.

§2 · interactive · current vs superseded

Which revision is current? Which is superseded?

Click a revision. The newest is current; every older one is superseded — do not use it.

This is the trap in Dialogue 2 and the voicemail: old paperwork shows a superseded revision.

§3 · grammar focus · indirect questions

Opener + statement order (+ whether / if)

DirectIndirect (polite + precise)
Which effectivity applies?Could you tell me which effectivity applies?
Is the bulletin mandatory?Could you clarify whether the bulletin is mandatory?
What is the current revision?Do you know what the current revision is?

Two rules: no do/does inside the clause · add whether/if for a yes/no question.

§3 · interactive · build the indirect question

From direct to polite — watch the word order

Choose a direct question, predict the polite form, then press Build. Watch do/does disappear and the verb stay after the subject.

Yes/no questions gain whether. This is the move every role-play needs.

§3 · Latvian / Russian interference

Three errors — and why they happen

✗ …which effectivity does apply? → …which effectivity applies?
L1 has no do/does helper — learners over-insert one. Remove it.

✗ …what is the current revision. → …what the current revision is.
Both L1s keep question inversion in embedded clauses. Force statement order.

✗ I want to know is my aircraft affected. → …whether my aircraft is affected.
Yes/no marked by intonation in L1 — the conjunction is dropped. Add whether/if.

§4 · reading · authentic genres

E5 Service Bulletin · E7 Work Order

SERVICE BULLETIN 34-2189, REV 3

Subject: pitot probe support bracket — replacement.

Applicability: A320, effectivity serials 5100–5140.

Compliance: recommended; no AD applies.

Parts: kit 34-2189-K3, lead time ~3 weeks.

WORK ORDER 22-0987 · RIGA

Aircraft YL-BAK · serial 5127.

Task: embody SB 34-2189, rev 3.

No wiring rework. Order kit today.

Sign-off: inspect, close panel, logbook entry.

Ask: find one fact that appears in BOTH texts. (serial / SB number / rev 3 / recommended)

§5 · speaking · four role-play cards

Call the OEM — A/B pairs, swap roles

CARD 1

Query an unclear effectivity. Is serial 5127 affected?

CARD 2

Mandatory or recommended? Confirm the deadline.

CARD 3

Confirm the next step: rework, lead time, logbook.

CARD 4

A superseded revision. Find the current one.

Model Card 1 first. Require ≥2 indirect questions + 1 read-back per turn. Score with the B2 rubric in the key.

§6 · writing · Simplified Technical English

A short, precise email to the OEM

Subject: SB 34-2189 — effectivity query, serial 5127

Three indirect questions · state the serial · ask for the applicability in writing · 90–120 words.

STE habits:

  • One idea per sentence.
  • Short sentences; no padding.
  • Clear subject line; active voice.
  • A written query joins the compliance record.
recap · close

Ask well · listen well · confirm before you act

Check effectivity first · give your serial early · mandatory vs recommended (an AD decides) · always the current revision · ask the lead time · record the embodiment in the logbook — quoting the SB and revision. The habit that carried it all: indirect questions — polite and precise.

When in doubt, ask for a clarification. Never guess. Homework: finish the STE email · self-check with the unit quiz.
