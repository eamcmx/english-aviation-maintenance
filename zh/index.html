What makes this different

Not an adapted textbook: materials written in Chinese for what these learners actually do.

Built for eating out and service Chinese

Every unit works a real situation of the field, in Chinese, with the documents, screens and conversations learners actually meet.

Four skills in every unit

Listening from studio-quality dialogues, vocabulary in context, grammar taught from the dialogues, staged practice, role-plays and a written deliverable.

Level-controlled by measurement

Every sentence is scored with the readability method for Chinese and must sit inside the CEFR band. Too hard and too easy both fail the gate.

Real Chinese audio, not read-aloud text

Two-voice studio dialogues generated for each unit, embedded in the page with transcripts revealed after listening.

Teacher-ready out of the box

Each unit ships a classroom deck, full answer key with rationale, staging and timing notes, L1 interference guidance and a CEFR speaking rubric.

A tutor in every page

The course buddy answers questions about each unit at the course level, and pronunciation practice scores the learner's spoken lines right in the browser.
