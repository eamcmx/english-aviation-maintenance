2 · Latvian interference — why each error happens

a · Dropped measure word — 我要一茶 → 我要一杯茶

Latvian counts nouns directly: viena tēja, desmit pelmeņi — there is no measure-word slot. Learners map that straight onto Chinese and skip 杯/个/碗. Fix: drill the number + measure word as one unstressed chunk (一杯…, 十个…, 一碗…) so the slot becomes automatic.

b · Fronted price word — 多少钱这个菜？ → 这个菜多少钱？

Latvian fronts question words: Cik maksā šis ēdiens? — "how-much" comes first. Chinese keeps normal topic–comment order and leaves 多少钱 at the end. Fix: teach the frame 「＿＿＿多少钱？」 and have learners slot the dish in front, never move 多少钱.

c · Extra 是 after 要/想 — 我是要米饭 / 我想是吃面条 → 我要米饭 / 我想吃面条

Latvian "es gribu rīsus" and the habit of an "is/am" copula tempt a linking 是. But 要 and 想 are already the verbs; 是 (to be) does not belong before another verb. Fix: contrast 我是学生 (是 + noun, ok) with 我要米饭 (要 = verb, no 是).

d · Pronunciation slips to pre-teach

  • 杯 bēi vs 啤 pí — b is soft/unaspirated, p is a puff of air. A wrong choice turns "a cup" into "beer". Hold a hand to the mouth: 啤 moves it, 杯 does not.
  • 钱 qián — q is a soft "tsj", not a hard k. 钱 (money) mispronounced becomes unclear at the till.
  • 想 xiǎng — x is a soft "sj"; not "ks" and not "sh".
  • 热 rè, 肉 ròu — the r does not roll; it is a soft, retroflex sound. Latvian's rolled r is the giveaway.

3 · Extension activities (fast finishers / a second lesson)

  • Kiosk order (Figure 3). In pairs, "use" the self-order screen: choose 米饭/面条, 鸡肉/牛肉, a drink, then read the 一共 total aloud. Stretches 要/想 + numbers.
  • Menu maths. Give three orders from the menu; learners total them and say 一共…块, then work out the 找 (change) from 100块. Pure spoken-number practice.
  • Mishearing game. One learner orders; the "waiter" deliberately mis-repeats one item; the customer must catch it with 不对，我要… — trains the repeat-back can-do.
  • Bigger table. Order for a group of three: each wants a different dish + drink; the waiter repeats the whole order back. Recycles measure words at volume.
  • AI partner. Learners run one role from the speaking cards with 小助手 (the page partner) and log two mistakes it recast for them.

4 · CEFR A2 speaking rubric — the role-plays

Score each strand 1–4 while monitoring the four cards. A2 target is a solid 3 across the row; a defended set of orders that a waiter could act on.

Strand4 · above A23 · at A2 (target)2 · approaching1 · below
Range Uses most bank words plus 还/也, several measure words, and connects two ideas. Orders a dish + a drink, asks 多少钱, uses 个/杯/碗 correctly for the core items. A few food words; only 个, or measure words dropped; limited drinks. Isolated words; cannot build a full order.
Accuracy Measure words, 要/想 and 多少钱-order nearly always right; slips self-repaired. Core patterns mostly right; the three L1 slips appear but do not block meaning. Frequent dropped measure words or fronted 多少钱; meaning sometimes unclear. Errors block the order; waiter cannot act on it.
Fluency Speaks in short, steady turns with little hesitation. Manages the order with some pausing; keeps going. Long pauses; needs prompts to continue each turn. Halting single words; stalls.
Interaction Asks and answers, checks back (…对不对？), reacts to the price. Responds to the waiter's questions and asks the price; takes turns. Answers only when asked; does not initiate the price question. No real exchange; parallel monologue.
Coherence Follows the sequence 看菜单→点菜→问多少钱→买单 and links with 还/也/然后. Keeps the ordering steps in a sensible order; order is complete. Steps jump around; an item or the payment is missing. No sequence; the scene does not resolve.
Repeat-back check (card 3). Give a bonus tick when the "waiter" repeats the whole order and closes with …对不对？ — that behaviour is the third can-do outcome.
