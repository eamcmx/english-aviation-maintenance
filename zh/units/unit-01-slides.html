Zhōngwén A2 · chīfàn hé fúwù · Unit 1 · Teacher deck

在餐厅：点菜和服务
Zài cāntīng: diǎn cài hé fúwù

At the restaurant — ordering & service. Order a meal and a drink, ask 多少钱, and take an order back. Restorānā: pasūtīt ēdienu un apkalpošana.

90 minutes · speaking · CEFR A2 · 个 / 杯 · 要 / 想 · 多少钱 · captions: press L

By the end · how the class runs

Three can-do outcomes — all spoken

1 · Order politely. Ask for a menu, order one dish + one drink with 要 / 想 and the right measure word.

2 · Ask & price. Ask what a dish is and 多少钱, and understand the answer in 块.

3 · Take & check. Take a simple order and repeat it back — …对不对？

Everything is practice: the dialogues, E1–E8, the four role-plays, the writing brief.

Scaffolding: every new word glossed in Latvian first use · a worked example before each task · a pronunciation clinic for the predictable slips.

§0 · the scenario

午餐时间 · lunchtime at 小海餐厅

You are hungry in Shanghai. A picture menu is on the table. 看菜单 → 点菜 → 问多少钱 → 买单.

Four steps, every time: read the menu, order, ask the price, pay. Today you also stand on the other side of the counter and take an order.

kàn càidān → diǎn cài → wèn duōshǎo qián → mǎidān

Order verbs: 要 yào (want, direct) · 想 xiǎng (would like, soft).

好吃 is for food. A drink is 好喝 hǎohē — do not say 好喝 about noodles.

§1 · Interactive · build an order

点菜 · click, then say the full sentence

Every click = one spoken sentence: 我要一碗米饭 · 我想喝一杯茶.

Then read the 一共 total in Chinese. The measure word is never optional — 一碗米饭, 一杯茶, 十个饺子.

§2 · grammar · 量词 measure words

A number needs a measure word

worked 一杯茶 yì bēi chá — a cup of tea → 杯 for drinks.

worked 十个饺子 shí ge jiǎozi — ten dumplings → 个 the general one.

一碗米饭 (碗 bowl) · 一份面条 (份 portion) · 一瓶啤酒 (瓶 bottle).

✗ 我要一茶

✓ 我要一杯茶

Latvian counts nouns directly (viena tēja) — so the 杯/个 slot is the first thing to drop. Drill the chunk 一杯…, 十个….

§2 · Interactive · does it fit?

量词 builder — green if the measure word fits

Drinks → 杯 · rice → 碗 · dumplings & dishes → 个 · a portion of noodles → 份 · bottles → 瓶.

Predict green or red before you reveal. Wrong pairs (一杯饺子) get a hint, not just a cross.

§2 · grammar · 要 yào & 想 xiǎng

Order with 要 or 想 — never add 是

worked 我要一碗米饭。 Wǒ yào yì wǎn mǐfàn. — direct order.

worked 我想吃饺子。 Wǒ xiǎng chī jiǎozi. — softer wish, 想 + verb.

✗ 我是要米饭　✗ 我想是吃面条

✓ 我要米饭　✓ 我想吃面条

要 and 想 are the verb — no linking 是. Compare 我是学生 (是 + noun, ok) with 我要米饭 (no 是).

§2 · Interactive · rAF · 多少钱 & spoken numbers

多少钱 goes last — and read the price in 块

✗ 多少钱这个菜？

✓ 这个菜多少钱？

Frame: ＿＿＿多少钱？ Slot the dish in front; keep 多少钱 at the end. Answer in 块 — 三十五块, 六十八块, 一百块.

§3 · Interactive · rAF · pronunciation clinic

发音 · where Latvian speakers slip

杯 bēi vs 啤 pí — b soft, p a puff of air. 钱 qián q = soft "tsj". 想 xiǎng x = soft "sj".

热 rè · 肉 ròu — the r does not roll. Trace each tone shape with your hand as it draws.

§4 · Dialogue 1 · 点午餐

Ordering from a picture menu

你好，欢迎光临，请问你要点菜吗？ …nǐ yào diǎn cài ma?
你好，请给我一张菜单，谢谢你。 qǐng gěi wǒ yì zhāng càidān…
你想吃米饭还是面条呢？ …mǐfàn háishì miàntiáo?
我想吃鸡肉面条，还要一个蔬菜。 …un vēl dārzeņus.
我要一杯热茶，不要咖啡，谢谢。 wǒ yào yì bēi rè chá…

Target: 一张菜单 · 想吃…还是… · 一个蔬菜 · 一杯热茶. Shadow, then read in pairs and swap.

§4 · Dialogue 2 · 问价钱和买单

Asking the dish & the price

服务员，请问一下，这个菜叫什么名字？
这个是鸡肉炒蔬菜，很新鲜也很好吃。 svaigs un garšīgs.
听起来很好吃，这个菜多少钱？ …duōshǎo qián?
这个菜三十五块，份量比较大。 sānshíwǔ kuài…
我吃完饭以后再买单，可以吗？ maksāt pēc ēšanas?
我们收信用卡，也收现金，都可以。

Numbers to drill: 三十五块 · 四十三块. Both come back in the listening quiz.

§4 · Dialogue 3 · 在柜台帮忙

Take an order — and repeat it back

我要两碗米饭和一份牛肉面条。
给我两杯茶和一瓶啤酒，谢谢你。
我重复一下你的菜，你听听对不对。 atkārtošu…
两碗米饭，一份牛肉面条，一份鸡肉饺子…对不对？
对，一共多少钱？ — 一共六十八块。
找你三十二块，请拿好，谢谢光临。 zhǎo nǐ sānshí'èr kuài

Closing move: …对不对？ Money: 68 → give 100 → change 32.

§4 · monologue · 餐厅广播

Listen for gist — the lunch announcement

Two questions before you play: 特别午餐多少钱？ · 牛肉面条要等几分钟？

Answers in the audio: set lunch 二十五块, a free 热茶 or 咖啡, beer 十块, beef noodles need 五分钟. Notice the glue words: 如果 (if), 然后 (then), 以后 (after).

§5 · exercises · controlled practice

E1–E4 · say the answer, then write it

E1 · 填空 — bank word into the gap. 要 · 咖啡 · 杯 · 多少钱 · 块…

E2 · 连线 — word → Latvian meaning.

E3 · 完成对话 — one word per gap to fix the exchange.

E4 · 改句子 — add the measure word / fix the order.

model E4 我要一茶 → 我要一杯茶.

Do E1 and E4 with the class — they carry the measure-word and word-order targets. E2/E3 can be pair-checked against the answers.

§5 · exercises · reading + listening

E5–E8 · menu, errors, note, re-listen

E5 · 阅读一 — read the menu, answer the prices in 块.

E6 · 找错误 — find & fix 8 errors in the waiter's notes.

E7 · 阅读二 — Anna's short note (gist).

E8 · 听力复习 — re-listen to Dialogue 3, answer the numbers.

model E5 牛肉面条多少钱？ → 22块.

E6 recycles every slip: dropped 杯/个, fronted 多少钱, extra 是, and 好喝 vs 好吃. Short on time → E7 becomes homework.

§6 · speaking · role-play rotation

Four cards · pairs · swap roles

1 · 点午餐 — order one dish + one drink. 请给我菜单 / 我要一份… / 我想喝一杯…

2 · 问价钱 — ask two dishes & the price. 这个菜叫什么？/ 这个多少钱？

3 · 服务员点菜 — take & repeat back. 你要点什么？/ 我重复一下…对不对？

4 · 买单 — ask the total & pay. 一共多少钱？/ 给你一百块。

Monitor with the A2 rubric (in the key): range · accuracy · fluency · interaction · coherence.

Note slips, recast one warmly at the end. Bonus tick for a full repeat-back with …对不对？ — that is outcome 3.

§7 · writing & AI partner

Self-check: measure word 个/杯? · 要 or 想? · asked 多少钱? · wrote hanzi + pinyin?
